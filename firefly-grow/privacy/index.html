Privacy Policy

Firefly Grow for iPhone · Effective 23 November 2026

Firefly Grow is a puzzle game. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not run our own servers. This page explains what data the game uses and who handles it.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (levels, XP, best times, daily puzzle streak, achievements, hints and settings) is saved only on your iPhone. We cannot see it. Deleting the app, or using Profile → Reset progress, removes it.

Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your total XP and weekly XP to Apple's leaderboards, where other players can see your Game Center nickname and score. Apple handles this data under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can turn Game Center off in the iPhone Settings app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free because it shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • information about the ads you see and tap, and basic diagnostic data.

Before any tracking, the game explains it and then shows Apple's permission prompt. If you choose not to allow tracking, you still get the same game; the ads are simply less personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices first, and you can change them later in Profile → Privacy choices.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details; the game only learns whether the purchase was made.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use any analytics service of our own.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children

Firefly Grow is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
