Support

Firefly Grow for iPhone

Found a bug, stuck on a level, or have an idea? Write to unforgiinq@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the version shown at the bottom of the Profile tab.

How do I play?

Tap a tile to place a lantern. A lantern lights its whole row and column until a stone blocks it. Two lanterns must never shine on each other, and a numbered stone needs exactly that many lanterns next to it. Light every tile to finish the level. You can also open Profile → How to play in the game.

Is every level solvable without guessing?

Yes. Every level is checked so it can be solved with logic alone. If you are stuck, a hint explains the next step.

How do I get more hints?

You start with free hints. When they run out, you can watch an optional short video for 3 more.

How do I remove ads?

Open Profile → Remove ads for a one-time purchase that removes the ads shown between levels. Optional videos for extra hints stay available if you want them. Bought it on another device? Use Profile → Restore purchases.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved on your iPhone only. Deleting the app or using Reset progress removes it, and it cannot be restored.

My Game Center score looks out of date

Apple can take a while to update leaderboards. Your score is sent each time you earn XP and again when you open the Contest tab.
